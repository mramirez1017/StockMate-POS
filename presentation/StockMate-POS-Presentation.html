The problem

The challenge most stores face

Money quietly leaks out through the gaps in manual stock-keeping.

  • Stock is a guess. No one knows the real on-hand count until something runs out — or expires.
  • Multiple branches, no single view. Each store keeps its own notebook or spreadsheet.
  • Shrinkage & errors. Price overrides, voids, and adjustments happen with no approval or trail.
  • Slow restocking. Reorders are reactive; deliveries are received without checks.
  • Reports take days to compile by hand — and are out of date by the time they're ready.

Result: lost sales, dead stock, theft, and decisions made on stale numbers.
