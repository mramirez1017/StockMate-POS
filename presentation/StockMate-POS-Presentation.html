Roles & access

The right access for every person

Role-based access keeps the system secure and uncluttered — each person sees only what they need, and sensitive actions require approval.

⬡ Store Owner
  • Full control of the business
  • All branches & reports
  • Approves requests
  • Manages users & pricing
◈ Branch Manager
  • Runs a branch end to end
  • Receiving & transfers
  • Requests price/stock changes
  • Branch-level reports
● Cashier
  • Point of sale & checkout
  • Search & scan products
  • Held & resumed sales
  • Customer transactions
◇ Platform Owner
  • Oversees multiple stores
  • Onboards businesses
  • System-wide governance
  • Top-level analytics

Managers request elevated permissions; the Owner approves or rejects — every action is captured in the audit trail.

The Screens

A guided tour of every page — what it shows, and who uses it.

Screen · Login
Secure sign-in
👤 All users

Login

What it does: Authenticates the user and loads the correct experience for their role and branch.
  • Email & password sign-in with show/hide
  • Role-aware — opens the right home screen
  • Branch context loaded automatically
  • Account lock for deactivated users
Screen · Dashboard
⬡ Owner · ◈ Manager

Dashboard

What it does: A live snapshot of the store — sales, alerts, and what needs attention today.
  • Today's sales, transactions, low-stock & critical
  • 7-day sales trend & sales-by-category
  • Top-selling products with performance change
  • Upcoming deliveries & branch transfers to act on
Live KPIs & charts
Screen · Point of Sale
Checkout & cart
● Cashier · ◈ Manager

Point of Sale

What it does: Fast, forgiving checkout — search or scan, build the cart, take payment, done.
  • Search by name/SKU or scan barcode
  • Live cart, manual discounts & auto tax
  • Split payments — cash, card, e-wallet
  • Hold & resume parked sales during rush
  • Stock deducted instantly on completion
Screen · Sales History
⬡ Owner · ◈ Manager

Sales History

What it does: A searchable record of every completed sale, with totals and payment details.
  • Filter by date, cashier, branch & payment
  • Summary cards: total sales, count, average
  • Status: completed, refunded, void
  • Drill into any invoice; export for accounting
All transactions
Screen · Products
Catalog & pricing
⬡ Owner · ◈ Manager

Products

What it does: The product catalog — names, SKUs, costs, prices, and pack/box definitions.
  • Identify by name & SKU (no photos needed)
  • Link category & supplier
  • Units per pack (e.g. 100 / box) for medicine-style goods
  • Cost & selling price with price-change approval
Screen · Categories & Suppliers

Catalog setup — Categories & Suppliers

What they do: The foundation data behind every product — how items are grouped, and who you buy them from. New requests can auto-suggest existing entries to prevent duplicates.
Categories — group products
Suppliers — vendor records
Screen · Inventory
⬡ Owner · ◈ Manager

Inventory

What it does: Live stock per branch, shown in the way your staff actually think about it.
  • Pieces & boxes together — "480 pcs (8 boxes)"
  • Color status: In Stock / Low / Out
  • Branch selector for per-location stock
  • Receive in boxes, sell in pieces — auto-converted
Live stock levels
Screen · Purchase Orders
Buy from suppliers
⬡ Owner · ◈ Manager

Purchase Orders

What it does: Order stock from suppliers and track each PO from draft to received.
  • Create POs per branch & supplier
  • Auto-reorder drafts from critical stock
  • Status: Draft → Pending → Approved → Received
  • Owner approval before ordering
Screen · Upcoming Deliveries
◈ Manager

Upcoming Deliveries

What it does: The receiving queue — everything heading into this branch in one place.
  • Branch transfers in transit with a Receive action
  • Incoming purchase orders with expected dates
  • Clear "what's arriving" picture for staff
  • One click to open the receive checklist
Incoming queue
Screen · Receive Delivery
Check-in goods
◈ Manager

Receive Delivery

What it does: Check delivered goods against the order and add them to stock accurately.
  • Expected qty shown as pcs & boxes
  • Enter boxes received → auto-converts to pieces
  • Flags shortages & mismatches
  • Per-piece cost derived from box cost on receive
Screen · Stock Transfers
◈ Manager requests · ⬡ Owner approves

Stock Transfers

What it does: Move stock between branches with a clear out → in-transit → received trail.
  • Manager requests; Owner approves
  • Owner-initiated transfers skip approval
  • Stock goes OUT of source, IN at destination on receive
  • Statuses: Pending, In Transit, Completed, Rejected
Branch-to-branch
Screen · Stock Adjustments
Correct stock
◈ Manager requests · ⬡ Owner approves

Stock Adjustments

What it does: Correct stock for damage, recounts or errors — with an approval gate.
  • Increase / decrease / damage with a reason
  • Requires approval (permission-controlled)
  • Full who/when/why on every change
  • Posts a stock movement for the audit trail
Screen · Stock Count
◈ Manager · ⬡ Owner

Stock Count

What it does: Run cycle counts, compare to the system, and post the difference.
  • Count sessions scoped by branch/category
  • System qty vs counted qty → variance
  • Summary of items counted & total variance
  • Post variance to reconcile stock instantly
Cycle counts & variance
Screen · Stock Disposal
Write-offs
◈ Manager · ⬡ Owner

Stock Disposal

What it does: Write off expired, damaged or spoiled goods and capture the value lost.
  • Reasons: Expired, Damaged, Spoiled
  • Records value lost for reporting
  • Removes items from sellable stock
  • Keeps shrinkage visible & accountable
Screen · Promotions
⬡ Owner

Promotions & Discounts

What it does: Create campaigns that apply automatically at checkout.
  • Percentage, fixed amount or Buy-1-Take-1
  • Scope to a product or category
  • Scheduled with start/end dates
  • Toggle active/inactive any time
Discounts & campaigns

Valuation & profit

Reports & Analytics

  • Inventory valuation & COGS
  • Gross profit over any date range
  • Stock movement & top sellers
  • Dead-stock / slow movers flagged
Screen · Activity & Approvals
⬡ Owner

Activity & Approvals

What it does: One place to approve pending requests and see a full audit trail.
  • Pending: permissions, price changes, transfers
  • Approve / reject with a click
  • Timeline of who did what, where & when
  • Complete audit log for accountability
Approvals & audit log

Request Detail

  • Approve/reject with an optional comment
Screen · Users & Roles
⬡ Owner

Users & Roles

  • Edit access level — granular permissions
  • Changes apply across the whole system instantly
Access control
